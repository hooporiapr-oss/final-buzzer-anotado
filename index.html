How a game raises money

Pledge. Play. Post. Paid.

Four steps, and the middle one is a basketball game you were already playing.

Pledge

Your players share the game. Each padrino promises a few cents per point — 10¢ to $1 — and nothing is owed until it is played.

Play

The team plays. No gym to book, no event to host, and no minimum anybody has to score for the night to count.

Post

The final score goes in once, on the scoreboard your tournament already uses. Every pledge is calculated from it.

Paid

Each padrino gets one notice with the final score and what their pledge came to. They settle it with your program the way they already would.

The other half of it

You already run the scoreboard. That is the platform.

marcadorpr holds the calendar, takes the scores and works out the table on its own. The bracket fills itself in — the moment a result lands, the winner shows up in the game they play next. It costs nothing to set up, and it runs whether anybody fundraises or not.

Which is why the pledging rides on top of it instead of beside it. A pledge button sits on a team’s own games, and the score that closes the game is the score that settles every pledge on it. Nobody types a number twice.

Where the money goes

The money never passes through us.

We record what was pledged and do the arithmetic. Your padrinos pay your program the way they already pay you — ATH Móvil, cash, however you arrange it. We are not in the middle of it.

We record every pledge, its rate and its cap
We calculate each total from the final score
We send each padrino a notice of what they owe your program
We do not process payments or hold anyone’s money
We do not take a percentage of what you raise
We charge your padrinos nothing at all

That last one is worth reading twice. A padrino’s pledge is what a padrino pays. Nothing is added on top of it.

What a padrino gets when a game closes is a notice: the final score and what their pledge came to. No payment link and no reminders — the payment is between them and your program.

Cada Punto Cuenta is strictly a pledge management software provider. Organizations independently manage all proceeds.

What it costs

From five dollars a game. Only the games with pledges on them.

The step is set by how many pledges a game gathered, and nothing else. Two games with twenty pledges cost the same whether one brought in a hundred dollars and the other ten thousand.

It does not move with your roster either, and a program signs up for five games to start.

  • No pledges, no fee. A game that gathers none has no step and is never invoiced.
  • Nothing up front. Signing up is free and running it is free.
  • Invoiced for the games that carried pledges, due within seven days.
  • Not a percentage of anything. It does not come out of any pledge, and your padrinos are charged nothing.
  • If it is not paid, that licence lapses and its pledge data comes off the platform. We do not chase it any further.
