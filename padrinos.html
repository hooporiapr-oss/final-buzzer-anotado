You score. Your padrinos decide what every point is worth.

The score is the same for everybody on the team. What is different, player to player, is how many people you asked — and you can start that today, before you touch a ball.

Every padrino raises what one of your points is worth

Each padrino pledges whatever they like, and your padrinos' pledges add together. That total is what one point the team scores is worth in your name — and it goes up every time somebody says yes.

Your Padrino Point Value

Every padrino's pledge, added together. That is what one team point is worth to you. Ten cents from one, two dollars from another — it all goes into the same number, and the number only ever goes up.

It is the part that is in your hands before anybody plays. The score is the team's; what a point is worth is yours.

About the ceiling

A padrino can set a ceiling — the most they will pay however the game goes. Plenty do, and it is a sensible thing to do. The system applies it, so what they are told at the end is never more than the number they chose.

This is the number to grow. The team scores what it scores whether you found two padrinos or twenty, so every padrino you find raises what each of those points is worth to you — in this game and in every one after it.

There is no amount too small to ask for

Ten cents a point is a real pledge. So is two dollars. People give what suits them, and none of them need to know what anyone else gave — they only ever see their own.

So do not decide for them. Ask everyone, let each one choose their own number, and watch what a point is worth climb.

One run, and it counts

A padrino picks a game, pledges, and pays the program after it is played — then picks another one if they want. Pledging on a game closes when that game starts.

Before the game Share your link One link, as many people as you can reach. Each one picks the game they want and what they pledge.
Game day Play Pledging on that game closes at tip-off. Nothing else for you to do — your coach provides the final score afterwards.
After Everyone sees the result Each padrino gets one email with the score and what their pledge came to. No payment link, and no reminders after it.

Win or lose, the points the team scored are the points the team scored. A padrino pays on those — there is no bonus for winning and nothing taken away for losing.

Who to ask

A padrino is anyone who would be glad you asked. Most players find ten without leaving the family.

Parents, and both sets of grandparents
Aunts, uncles, older cousins
Godparents, and your parents' close friends
Neighbours you actually know
A coach, a teacher, a former teacher
Family who live far away — distance does not matter
A business your family knows — where your parents work, the shop on the corner

The ones who live furthest away are often the quickest to say yes. They cannot come to anything, and this is something they can be part of.
